A one-touch game for iPhone and iPad

Blow a seed
into the sky.

You are a dandelion seed. Tap anywhere and a gust blows from your finger, pushing the seed away from it. Tap below to lift it, beside it to steer, and hold for a deep breath. Climb as high as the sky allows, and wherever your seed comes to rest, a flower grows in your garden.

Get support

Six layers of sky.

Drift up from a sunny meadow through brambles, rain, a canopy of webs and swallows, a lightning storm and, if you're good, twilight under the aurora. The sky keeps rising, so keep moving.

Breath

Three breaths that refill slowly. Golden pollen refills them at once.

Updrafts & butterflies

Ride warm air and catch a butterfly for a lift.

Five seeds

Dandelion, Milkweed, Thistle, Maple and Cottonwood each fly differently.

Your garden

Every flight grows a flower. Climb higher for rarer blooms.

Play free. Remove ads once.
