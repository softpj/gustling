Your privacy.

This policy describes the Gustling iOS game developed by Arun Bhardwaj. For support, privacy questions or requests, contact softpj@icloud.com.

Progress and settings

Your garden, seeds, pollen, best climb and preferences are saved locally on your device. Gustling does not require an account and has no developer-operated game server. Deleting the app may erase local progress.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google AdMob for a banner above the game and occasional interstitial ads between flights. Google's advertising SDK may process device identifiers, approximate location inferred from your IP address, ad impressions and interactions, and crash and performance information. These support advertising, measurement, analytics and SDK reliability. Processing depends on your consent, tracking permission and Google's settings.

Where required, the app presents consent choices and provides an Ad privacy options control in Settings. iOS tracking permission can be changed in system Settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's SDK data disclosures.

Purchases

Apple processes Remove Ads payments. Gustling checks Apple-verified transactions to unlock and restore ad removal. We do not receive payment-card details. Purchasing stops future advertising requests; it does not delete information already held by service providers.

Game Center

If you choose to use Apple Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center profile and scores under its own privacy practices.

Retention and choices

You can remove locally stored data by deleting the app. Service providers manage data they receive under their own policies; contact the relevant provider for requests about that data. Support emails are used to answer your request and kept only as long as needed.

Children

Gustling is a general-audience game and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly request children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to support.

This website

These pages have no advertising, analytics scripts or contact form. Your browser and the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process technical request information to deliver the site.

Updates

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date above shows the latest version.
