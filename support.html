Common questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere and a gust blows from your finger, pushing the seed away. Tap just below the seed to lift it and beside it to steer; closer taps blow harder. Hold for a deep breath and a bigger gust, which uses two breaths.

What are the leaves under my seed?

Your breath. Each gust uses one (a deep breath uses two). They refill slowly, and golden pollen refills them all at once.

Why did my run end?

The sky keeps rising. If your seed sinks off the bottom of the screen, snags on thorns, is snapped up by a swallow or is struck by lightning, it comes to rest and a flower grows where it landed.

How do I get new seeds and flowers?

Collect pollen during flights and spend it on new seeds in the Seeds screen. Flowers depend on how high you climb: daisy, poppy, bluebell, foxglove, moonflower and, above 1,080 metres, the rare starbloom.

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Sign in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase, then open Gustling → Settings → Restore purchases.

I paid, but I still see ads.

Let any pending Apple approval finish, then try Restore purchases with the original Apple Account. If the problem continues, email support. Please don't buy again to troubleshoot.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments and refunds. Visit Apple's Report a Problem website. We can't see your card details or issue App Store refunds directly.

Where is my progress stored?

Your garden, seeds, pollen and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove them. Remove Ads can be restored separately through Apple.

Can I change my advertising choices?

Where required, open Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also manage tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
